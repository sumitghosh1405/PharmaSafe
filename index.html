Responsible interpretation

FDA states that adverse-event reports are not extensively validated and do not by themselves establish causality. A single report may list several drugs and reactions without linking a specific drug to a specific outcome. This tool therefore labels every result a statistical screening signal, never a confirmed safety finding — and every ROR/PRR calculation ships with its 95% confidence interval so the uncertainty is visible, not hidden in a single number.
