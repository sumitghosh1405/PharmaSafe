ABOUT THE PROJECT

PharmaSafe

PharmaSafe is a web-based pharmacovigilance research workspace that helps users explore public FDA adverse-event data through focused analysis, comparison, signal screening, case review, and personal research organization.

The problem it addresses

Spontaneous-reporting datasets are rich but difficult to explore directly. A researcher may need to move between drug names, reaction terms, report records, time patterns, and statistical screening methods. PharmaSafe brings those exploratory tasks into one interface while keeping the underlying limitations visible.

What the product includes

Drug analysis

Explore reactions and report-level summaries for a medicinal product.

Comparison

Place two medicines side by side using the application's existing comparison workflow.

Signal screening

Use ROR and PRR as disproportionality screening measures with uncertainty shown.

Research Board

Keep medicines currently being studied together and reopen or compare them later.

Data provenance

The application's adverse-event workflows use public FDA-derived data exposed through openFDA. The project links back to the primary documentation so users can inspect how the source describes its data and limitations.

Who it is for

The interface is intended for learning, exploratory pharmacovigilance research, and safety-data analysis workflows. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice or a regulatory safety assessment.
