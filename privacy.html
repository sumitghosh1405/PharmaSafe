LEGAL & PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

This page describes the categories of information PharmaSafe is designed to handle. It should be reviewed and adapted to the actual hosting, analytics, Firebase, and advertising configuration before being treated as a final legal policy.

Account information

When an account is created, PharmaSafe may process the name and email address supplied for authentication. Authenticated Research Board and activity information is intended to be associated with the user's account.

Research workspace data

The Research Board and recent-analysis records are designed to be user-scoped. The application should only expose a user's personal workspace to that authenticated user.

Analytics and advertising

The production application may use web analytics and advertising services. Those services can process technical information according to their own policies and configuration. Users should review the applicable Google privacy documentation and the site's current consent configuration.

Public data

FDA/openFDA adverse-event data displayed by PharmaSafe is public source data and is not user-submitted personal health information collected by PharmaSafe through the analysis tools.

Contact and policy updates

This policy should be updated whenever the site's data collection, analytics, authentication, advertising, or hosting configuration changes. Nothing on this page overrides applicable law or third-party service terms.
